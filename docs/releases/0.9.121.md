## Scope and source

Since macOS 0.9.120 (`4d36bd76ae7343c5068fe15e0be1efc33ba79535`):

- **#490, comment highlight on dual-orientation streams (plan 074).** With a
  vertical simulcast leg, the highlight card reached no output while the
  backend reported it live (broken since 0.9.94). It now burns on the
  horizontal primary and, with a portrait raster, on the vertical leg;
  availability follows the leg plan. Owner acceptance on a real dual stream
  is owed.
- **#491, named followers with avatars (plan 071).** Depends on
  videorc-web #56 and #57 (merged). The X `follow.follow` subscription is
  unproven until the first Go Live to X; if X refuses it, X stays
  count-only.
- **#488, glass floating surfaces (plan 072).** Owner by-eye pass and a
  Windows look are owed.
- **#487, live account refresh (plan 073).** Includes the lazy-loaded
  Windows live-audio smoke harness that freed eager renderer bytes.
- **#489,** eager renderer bundle back under budget after #484.
- #492 (bump). The unpublished `0.9.120-alpha.1` entry moved to
  `0.9.121-alpha.1`, following the #485 precedent.

## Owner decisions (2026-09-28)

- "make now a new release". macOS follows the regular Beta scope (build,
  sign, notarize, staple, artifact validation; device, endurance and
  clean-machine gates not run). #487, #488, #490 and #491 ship before their
  owner acceptance, by that decision.
