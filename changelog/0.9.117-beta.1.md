## Orcle hears you

Orcle used to know only what was in chat, or what you said while live
captions were on. Turn on **Orcle hears you while you're live** in
Settings → Orcle, or answer the card the first time you open Orcle, and it
follows what you say for the whole stream, with captions on or off.

Your microphone audio goes to Videorc's cloud speech-to-text to be turned
into text. Videorc's servers don't keep it, and the transcript is saved with
your recording on this computer. Silence is never sent, for captions too.
Listening has its own monthly allowance, shown under the switch, and a
Listening indicator tells you in plain words when Orcle can't hear you.
Listening never blocks or stops a recording or stream.

## Clip that

Say "clip that", or press **Mark clip** in the Stream Manager, to mark the
last 30 seconds. You can also set a shortcut for it in Settings → Shortcuts,
or send it from the phone remote or a Stream Deck. Your marks show first in
Publish → Clips. A mark needs Record on; with Record off, nothing is saved
and Videorc tells you why.

## Orcle's regular check

Orcle's check now hears you as well as chat. It shows what you're talking
about and marks the questions that are on topic, sorted first. When you
promise something on stream, like a giveaway at 100 viewers or a Q&A in ten
minutes, Orcle keeps it under Promises and reminds you when the moment
comes, or after 20 minutes. When a viewer asks what they missed, a recap
card is ready; **Post to chat** puts it in the chat box for you to edit, and
never sends it by itself. The chat label that read "Orcle listening" now
reads "Orcle is reading chat".

## Say hi and quiet moments

**Say hi** lists first-time chatters you haven't greeted. Say their name,
reply in chat, put their comment on stream or tick them off by hand, and they
leave the list. If you go quiet for 20 seconds while a question or a new
chatter is waiting, Orcle gives you a private nudge. It never does while you
are muted.

## Your pack, made for you

After a recorded stream that Orcle heard, your Publish pack runs once on its
own and a toast offers **Open Publish**. Chapters get real timestamps from
what you said, and the transcript is saved with the recording as subtitles,
even with captions off. Turn this off with **Make my post-stream pack
automatically** in Publish.
