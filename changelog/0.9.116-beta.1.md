## Kick chat

If Kick chat can't connect, the Stream Manager used to say "Reconnecting"
for the whole stream and never explain. After a few failed tries, it now
tells you what is wrong: Videorc's chat relay is down, Kick refused the
connection, or there is no internet connection. Videorc keeps retrying on
its own either way.

The Stream Manager also shows more from Kick. New subs and renewals, gifted
subs, KICKs and bans now land in chat and Activity next to follows. KICKs
are listed one gift at a time. A ban with an end time reads as a timeout.

Until Kick marks your channel as live, the Kick viewer count reads
"waiting" instead of showing zero viewers.

## Warnings

A warning that a recording or stream is having trouble now disappears when
the session ends. A late warning from an earlier session no longer shows up
during the next one.
