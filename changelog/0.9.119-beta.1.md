## System audio on or off

Videorc never captured what your Mac was playing. The old System Audio mixer
row was only a placeholder and was removed earlier. This Beta puts a real
**System audio** switch in Studio.

Turn it **On** and recordings and every stream leg include the computer's
sound: browser tabs, games, music, calls, whatever is playing. Turn it **Off**
and only your microphone (and other sources you chose) stay in the mix. The
default is **Off**, so nothing changes until you flip the switch.

You can flip it mid-session. The Studio mixer shows a live meter while System
audio is on. Sources → Audio mixer adds a gain slider (default −6 dB). If
Screen Recording permission is missing, or capture cannot start, the row tells
you in plain words and the session keeps going without system audio.

## Shortcut, phone and Stream Deck

Bind **Turn system audio on/off** in Settings → Global shortcuts, toggle it
from the phone remote on Wi-Fi, or map a Stream Deck action. You never have to
leave the stream to change it.

## What stays out

Your microphone is still its own row. Videorc's own interface sounds and
Orcle's voice are kept out of System audio capture, so turning it on does not
feed the app back into itself.
