## Highlighted comments on vertical streams

When you streamed to a vertical destination (YouTube Shorts, TikTok,
Instagram) at the same time as a horizontal one, a highlighted comment showed
as **On stream** in Stream Manager but never appeared on either video.

Highlighting now puts the comment card on your horizontal stream and on your
vertical stream. The vertical card is sized for a phone screen, so it reads
as clearly as the horizontal one. Both use the corner you picked in the Chat
window, and both clear together. Stream Manager only says **On stream** when
viewers can actually see the card.

## New followers, by name

Activity now shows who followed you, with their avatar, instead of a generic
"New follower" row. Follows, subs, gifts, tips and raids all show the person
and their platform. **Thank in chat** mentions their handle so the @-mention
works.

- **Twitch:** click **Show who followed** once to allow Videorc to see
  follower names. It takes effect on the stream you are in.
- **Kick:** follow rows show the follower's avatar.
- **X:** new followers are named when X delivers them; otherwise X shows a
  count as before.

## Floating surfaces match the glass

Hover cards, popovers, menus, selects, tooltips, dialogs, the command
palette and toasts now use the same glass as the window, one step lighter so
they stand out, instead of near-black panels. Success and error toasts keep
their color in the icon and edge rather than filling the whole toast.

## Refresh while live

Refreshing Sources or Settings during a recording or stream no longer shows
"Account maintenance is deferred" and no longer skips the refresh. A camera
you plug in mid-stream now shows up. Account details catch up automatically
once the session ends.
